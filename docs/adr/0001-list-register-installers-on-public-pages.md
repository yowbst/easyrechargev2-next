# List register installers on public pages

Commune and canton pages name Installers from the commercial register, sole proprietorships included, even though most are not Partners and the same pages collect Leads. We accepted the risk of sending visitors to potential competitors because the register is public, other directories already show it, and local proof is what these pages lack in search ("installation borne de recharge {commune}" had impressions and no clicks). Decided 2026-11-21, live 2026-11-22.

Safeguards that are part of the decision:

- Active Partners always come first, with the quote button. Other Installers get no phone, e-mail or website: name, street address without care-of names, distance, register link.
- Every list states its source and date and offers removal through the contact page. A company that asks is Hidden.
- Only Installers with explicit installation wording in their registered purpose are Listed; liquidation never is.
- Rolled out in Vaud first, with the other served cantons as comparison; switched off if the Vaud quote rate on commune pages drops more than 15 %.
