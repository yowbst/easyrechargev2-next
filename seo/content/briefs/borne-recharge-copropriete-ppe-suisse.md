## Outline

- **H1:** Borne de recharge en copropriété (PPE) en Suisse : majorité, coûts et étapes
- **TL;DR block** (3 sentences): utile work → double majority; ~CHF 500-1'500 per space for the shared base installation + ~CHF 2'000-3'500 per charger; plan 3 to 9 months; no legal right to charge in force yet.
- **H2 Peut-on installer une borne en PPE sans l'accord des autres copropriétaires ?** Direct answer: no in a shared garage (common parts belong to the community; work without consent can be ordered removed); exceptions: closed box with no work on common parts, or a right already in the règlement. Tenants: written consent of the landlord (art. 260a CO).
- **H2 Quelle majorité faut-il pour voter l'installation ?** Table: nécessaire (647c) / utile (647d, double majority, the usual classification per the OFEN guide and SVIT) / somptuaire (647e). Mandating the preliminary study: simple majority. Changing the règlement (712g al. 3) and special use rights (712g al. 4).
- **H2 Combien coûte une installation de recharge en PPE ?** Per-space table (OFEN guide p. 13): base installation 500-1'500 (life > 20 years), charger 2'000-3'500 (life ~8 years), management/billing 0-15 per month. Worked example: 40-space garage, base installation 25'000-32'000 (625-800 per space), charger + connection 2'100-2'500 per space. Geneva reference price (SIG). Link to the cost post for houses.
- **H2 Qui paie quoi ? Les 5 variantes de financement** Table V1-V5 with the guide's recommendation (V1/V2), V4 as fallback (conflict risk, later joiners pay the depreciated value), V5 only for a few spaces. Renovation fund: tax note (p. 20). Common electricity must not pay for charging.
- **H2 Quelles sont les étapes, de l'idée à la mise en service ?** The guide's 4 steps; 3 to 9 months; assembly documents checklist; final inspection and safety report.
- **H2 Qu'est-ce qu'une infrastructure « prête pour la recharge » (niveaux A à D) ?** SIA 2060 levels in plain words; why the base installation for all spaces + load management avoids a costly grid reinforcement; 11 kW AC; MID meters.
- **H2 Comment facturer l'électricité entre copropriétaires ?** Cost keys (units / quotes-parts), separate metering and re-billing, systems overview (recharge-au-point.ch).
- **H2 Existe-t-il un droit à la recharge en Suisse ?** Not in force. Motion 23.3936 (adopted by the Council of States 24-18 on 2025-06-11); draft art. 45c LEne in consultation until 2026-10-12: base installation on request "si elle peut raisonnablement être imposée", charger at the user's cost. Say clearly it is a draft and date the statement.
- **H2 Genève et Vaud : obligations et subventions** GE: RPSFP art. 5 al. 6 (underground car parks equipped for charging, load management from 5 points); GE subsidy CHF 500/300/250 per space, cap 20'000 per car park, max 50 %. VD: LVLEne art. 42 from 2027-01-01 (new buildings and heavy renovations, ≥ 3 dwellings); VD subsidy CHF 200-400 per space, cap 100'000. Links to the cantonal guides and {r:localities}.
- **H2 Locataire en immeuble : que faire ?** 260a CO in three points (written consent; restoration only if agreed in writing; indemnity for a considerable added value); possible rent increase if the landlord pays. Link to the tenant post.
- **H2 Questions fréquentes** (5): Un copropriétaire peut-il bloquer le projet ? Faut-il équiper toutes les places ? Combien de temps dure le projet ? Qui paie l'électricité de recharge ? Une borne augmente-t-elle les charges de PPE ?

## Unique Angle

The only page that puts the Swiss legal rules, per-space costs from the federal guide, financing variants and the 2026 legislative status in one readable place, for an owner who has to convince an assembly. Clearly separates law in force from drafts, and Switzerland from France.

## Takeaways

- Installing charging in a shared garage is usually a "useful" work: it needs a majority of owners who also hold more than half of the shares (art. 647d CC).
- Budget ~CHF 500-1'500 per space for the shared base installation, then ~CHF 2'000-3'500 per charger (OFEN guide, 2026).
- No right to charge is in force in Switzerland yet; a federal draft (art. 45c LEne) was in consultation until October 2026.

## Expert Advice

Start with the cheapest decision: a simple-majority vote to fund a needs survey and a building study. It turns a debate about one owner's car into a plan for the whole garage, sizes the load management for every space, and gives the assembly real quotes to vote on.
